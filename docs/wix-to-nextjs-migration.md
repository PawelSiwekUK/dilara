# Moving Dilara's Finesse Shop from Wix to Next.js

Customer-facing summary. Timeline and costs are **estimates** and should be confirmed once the scope is agreed.

## What we'll do

1. **Audit the current store.** List every product, collection, page, URL, plugin and integration (payments, shipping, email, analytics).
2. **Migrate the data.** Export products, images, categories, customers and order history from Wix. Import them into the new store backend.
3. **Choose the backend.** Options include Shopify, Stripe with a database, or a headless CMS such as Medusa or Sanity. Payments go through Stripe or PayPal.
4. **Build the storefront.** Next.js pages for home, catalogue, product, cart, checkout, account and content pages. The design matches your current brand.
5. **Keep your search ranking.** Redirect every old Wix URL (301) to its new address. Carry over metadata, sitemap and structured product data.
6. **Connect the services.** Shipping, tax, order emails, analytics, cookie consent and legal pages.
7. **Test.** Checkout and payment tests, mobile checks, speed and accessibility checks.
8. **Launch.** Point the domain DNS at the new site. Keep Wix running until the switch, then monitor for the first few weeks.

## Benefits

- **Faster pages.** Server rendering and image optimisation improve load times, which tends to lift conversion and Google rankings.
- **Better SEO.** Full control over metadata, URLs and structured data.
- **Lower running costs.** No Wix plan fees or Wix's extra transaction charges. You pay only for hosting.
- **Full ownership.** You own your code and data, with no platform lock-in.
- **Flexibility.** Custom features such as bundles, discounts, loyalty schemes and integrations are not limited by the Wix app store.
- **Scalability.** Handles traffic spikes without plan upgrades.
- **A better mobile experience.** It behaves like a modern web app.

## What we need from you

## Risks and how we handle them

- **Temporary SEO dip.** Reduced by redirects and a sitemap.
- **Customer accounts.** Passwords can't be exported from Wix, so customers will need to reset theirs.
- **Downtime.** None expected, because we switch over only once the new site is tested.
